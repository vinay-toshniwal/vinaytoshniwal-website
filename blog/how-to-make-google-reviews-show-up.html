How to Make Google Reviews Show Up: A Simple Trick That Works

Your customer left a 5-star review, but it never appeared. Here's exactly why that happens and the simple fix that makes reviews show up instantly.

February 17, 2025
Vinay Toshniwal
Reviews
GBP Tips & Tricks

Why Are Google Reviews Important for Local Businesses?

For businesses that serve local customers, Google Business Profile (GBP) is a powerful tool for attracting potential clients. It helps businesses rank higher in Google Search and Google Maps, increasing trust and credibility.

However, what happens when a satisfied client leaves a review, and it never appears on your profile?

Customer reviews are one of the strongest ranking signals for local SEO, and missing reviews can negatively impact your online credibility, local search rankings, and potential conversions.

I ran an experiment to fix this issue, and the results were surprising.

The Problem: Missing Reviews from Distant Clients

A client located 16 hours away left a 5-star review sharing their positive experience. However, the review never showed up on my Google Business Profile.

This is a common issue caused by Google's review filtering system, which hides reviews that seem suspicious or unverified.

Common Reasons Why Google Reviews Disappear

  • The reviewer is located far from the business location (out of state or country)
  • The review comes from a new Google account with no review history
  • The review was submitted without prior engagement with the business
  • Multiple reviews were left within a short period
  • The review contains links, promotional content, or identical text to another review

The Simple Trick That Makes Reviews Show Up Instantly

Since I knew my client's review was genuine, I tested a simple workaround, and it worked immediately.

Step-by-step method:

  1. Ask the client to delete their original review.
  2. Instruct them to search for your business on Google.
  3. Have them click the "Call" button on your GBP listing and stay on the line for about one minute.
  4. After that, have them resubmit the same review.
The Result
The review appeared instantly ✅

By making this small engagement before leaving a review, Google was able to verify the reviewer's connection to the business, preventing it from being flagged as spam.

Why Does This Trick Work?

Google's algorithm prioritizes real customer interactions before publishing a review. If a user calls, visits, or engages with your business before leaving a review, it signals authenticity and lowers the chances of the review being filtered.

How Google Identifies Authentic Reviews

  • The customer interacted with your GBP before leaving a review (called, visited, or messaged)
  • The review contains real experiences, specific details, or images
  • The customer has left reviews on other businesses (this builds credibility in Google's eyes)

Additional Tips to Prevent Reviews from Disappearing

  • Ask customers to leave reviews while they are at your location
  • Encourage them to add photos or detailed descriptions to their review
  • Make sure they don't copy-paste identical reviews across multiple businesses
  • Avoid incentivizing reviews: Google detects and penalizes unnatural patterns

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do some Google reviews never show up?

Google's review filtering system hides reviews it cannot verify, especially when the reviewer is located far from the business, uses a new Google account with no review history, left the review without prior engagement with the business, posted multiple reviews in a short period, or the review contains links, promotional content, or text that is identical to another review.

What is the simple trick to make a filtered review show up?

Ask the client to delete their original review, search for your business on Google, click the "Call" button on your Google Business Profile listing and stay on the line for about one minute, then resubmit the same review.

Why does calling the business before leaving a review help?

Google's algorithm prioritizes real customer interactions before publishing a review. If a user calls, visits, or engages with the business before leaving a review, it signals authenticity and lowers the chances of the review being filtered.

How can businesses prevent reviews from disappearing in the future?

Ask customers to leave reviews while they are at your location, encourage them to add photos or detailed descriptions, make sure they don't copy-paste identical reviews across multiple businesses, and avoid incentivizing reviews since Google detects and penalizes unnatural patterns.

Final Thoughts

If your business relies on Google reviews for credibility and local SEO, it's essential to ensure they actually show up. Try this simple trick and let me know if it worked for you!
