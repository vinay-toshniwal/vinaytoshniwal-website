A quick precaution for anyone managing Google Business Profiles. September 2026 Vinay Toshniwal Account Safety Okay, Google is now after email accounts too. Fair enough. Just a quick precaution for anyone managing Google Business Profiles. If you are managing important GBPs under a single Google account, I would avoid keeping that as the only owner or manager. • Add multiple trusted email IDs as owners or managers. These can be your own accounts or accounts belonging to trusted friends, family members, or team members. • Even better, use a Google Workspace account with your own domain rather than depending entirely on free Gmail accounts.
