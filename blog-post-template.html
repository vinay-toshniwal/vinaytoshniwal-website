How to Fix a Suspended Google Business Profile: Complete DIY Guide

This is a ready single-post page that matches your homepage styling. Replace the text below with your full original article whenever you want.

May 4, 2025
DIY Guide
Google Business Profile

If your Google Business Profile is suspended, the first step is not to panic. Most businesses lose time because they appeal too quickly without fixing the actual issue that triggered the suspension.

This template is structured like a real blog article, so you can paste your original content directly into it and publish it as part of the same website.

Start with a full profile check

Before doing anything else, review your business name, address, phone number, category, service area, website, and supporting documents. Even small mismatches can hurt the appeal.

Tip: Fix the profile first. Appeal second. Businesses often fail because they submit an appeal while the same issue still exists.

Things to review before submitting an appeal

  • Make sure the business name follows Google guidelines.
  • Check that the address and service area setup are correct.
  • Use a working website and matching contact details.
  • Prepare clear business proof before you submit the appeal.
