Common Causes

Why Google Business Profiles Get Suspended

Google suspends profiles for a wide range of policy violations, many of which business owners don't even know exist.

📋

Incorrect Business Information

Name, address or phone number doesn't match what's on your website or other directories.

⚠️

Category or Service Violations

Choosing restricted categories or adding services that conflict with Google's content policies.

🔐

Verification Problems

Failed or incomplete verification, or attempting to verify a location that doesn't qualify.

🤖

Algorithmic Flags

Automated systems flagging unusual activity patterns, bulk edits, or sudden information changes.

🏢

Address or Location Issues

Using an ineligible address, virtual office, co-working setup, or location details that Google cannot verify properly.

✏️

Frequent Profile Edits

Multiple sudden changes to name, category, address, phone number, or services can trigger trust and compliance checks.

How does your Google Business Profile Reinstatement Service work, and how much does it cost?

I specialize in reinstating suspended Google Business Profiles, including difficult cases where an appeal or additional review has already been rejected.

Before proceeding, I conduct a complete audit of your profile and business presence to identify potential Google Business Profile guideline violations. This can include your business name, address, phone number, website, categories, service areas, supporting documents, signage, citations, and other eligibility requirements.

I then guide you through the required corrections, documentation, verification, appeal, and escalation process until the case is resolved.

Pricing: USD 500 to USD 1,000 on success, depending on the category and violation type. You pay only after your Google Business Profile is successfully reinstated. In some cases I may request an advance, but if the reinstatement is unsuccessful, that advance is refunded in full. Certain industries or unusually complex cases may have different pricing, which will always be discussed before I begin.
Do you take every case?

No. I only take a case when the business is legitimate and you can provide the required documents. If something looks fishy, I decline it right away and do not work on it.

How does your Negative Review Removal Service work, and how much does it cost?

This service is for Google reviews that may violate Google’s review policies, including certain fake, spam, abusive, defamatory, or otherwise policy violating reviews.

I first review the content of the review, reviewer activity where relevant, and the available evidence to determine whether there is a reasonable policy basis for removal. If there is, I handle the reporting and available escalation process.

A genuine negative review cannot be removed simply because you disagree with it. Google makes the final decision on whether a review violates its policies.

Pricing: Generally USD 500 per review. Depending on the case and the work involved, this may be charged as a non refundable fee upfront or only after successful removal. The exact structure is confirmed with you before I begin.
How does your Spam Business Profile Removal Service work, and how much does it cost?

I help identify and report Google Business Profiles that appear to violate Google’s guidelines, including fake businesses, ineligible listings, misleading information, fake locations, and other forms of Google Maps spam.

I review the profiles, investigate the potential violations, collect supporting evidence where available, and use the appropriate reporting channels to request action from Google.

Google makes the final decision about whether a listing should be edited, restricted, or removed, so removal cannot be guaranteed.

Pricing: Contact me to discuss pricing, which depends on the number of profiles and complexity of each case.
Do you provide White Label Google Business Profile Reinstatement for agencies, and what does it cost?

Yes. My White Label Reinstatement Service is designed for agencies that manage Google Business Profiles but want a specialist to handle difficult suspension and reinstatement cases behind the scenes.

Your agency can maintain its relationship with the client while I handle the audit, troubleshooting, compliance corrections, documentation guidance, appeals, and reinstatement process.

This allows agencies to offer GBP reinstatement without having to build an internal troubleshooting team or refer their clients elsewhere.

Pricing: USD 500 per Google Business Profile. This may increase for certain categories or unusually complex cases, which will be discussed before I begin.
What is your GBP Compliance & Protection Service, and how much does it cost?

This service is available to clients whose Google Business Profile I have successfully reinstated. Once your profile is back online, you become eligible to enroll in ongoing compliance protection.

I monitor and help keep your profile compliant so it does not get suspended again. If your profile is suspended again while enrolled, I fix it at no additional cost.

This is simply protection against suspension. It does not include the audit, guideline review, or preventive guidance covered under other services; it is focused specifically on keeping a reinstated profile safe and fixing it again if needed.

Pricing: USD 100 per month or USD 1,200 per year.
What is included in your Google Business Profile Live Training, and how much does it cost?

I provide one to one live Google Business Profile training for business owners, agencies, SEO professionals, and teams that want practical knowledge about GBP rather than only theoretical information.

The session can cover Google Business Profile guidelines, suspensions, reinstatement, verification, service area businesses, review issues, profile eligibility, common mistakes, troubleshooting, and suspension prevention. The session can also focus on your specific questions or real GBP cases.

I have been working with Google Business Profile since 2016 and use my experience from troubleshooting real cases to make the training practical and relevant.

Pricing: USD 1,000 for a 240 minute live session, including 1 year of email or message support to answer your follow up questions.
