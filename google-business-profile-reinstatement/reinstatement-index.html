Why Google Business Profiles Get Suspended

Google suspends profiles for a wide range of policy violations, many of which business owners don't even know exist. See the full DIY guide for a detailed breakdown of each suspension type.

📋
Incorrect Business Information
Name, address or phone number doesn't match what's on your website or other directories.
⚠️
Category or Service Violations
Choosing restricted categories or adding services that conflict with Google's content policies.
🔐
Verification Problems
Failed or incomplete verification, or attempting to verify a location that doesn't qualify.
🤖
Algorithmic Flags
Automated systems flagging unusual activity patterns, bulk edits, or sudden information changes.
🏢
Address or Location Issues
Using an ineligible address, virtual office, co-working setup, or location details that Google cannot verify properly.
✏️
Frequent Profile Edits
Multiple sudden changes to name, category, address, phone number, or services can trigger trust and compliance checks.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Google Business Profile Reinstatement FAQs

If your question isn't answered here, get in touch directly; I respond within 24 hours.

My Google Business Profile is suspended. Can you get it reinstated?

If your business is legitimate and eligible for a Google Business Profile, I can help with the Google Business Profile reinstatement process.

I have been working with Google Business Profiles since 2016 and have handled many complex suspension and reinstatement cases.

Before starting, I review your Business Profile, website, business information, and supporting documents. The goal is not simply to get your profile reinstated, but to get it live, verified, compliant, and with your existing reviews intact.

Do you work with every suspended Google Business Profile?

No. I only work with legitimate businesses that are eligible for a Google Business Profile.

Before taking your case, I review the business and available evidence. If I believe the business is not eligible or does not have sufficient proof of legitimate operations, I may not accept the case.

I do not help create, verify, or reinstate fake or ineligible Business Profiles, regardless of the amount offered.

Google rejected my Business Profile appeal. Can you still help?

Yes. A rejected Google Business Profile appeal does not necessarily mean the end of the road.

I also work on complex cases where the initial appeal and even an additional review have already been rejected.

I review the complete case, identify potential compliance issues, correct what needs to be fixed, and determine the appropriate next steps for getting the Business Profile reinstated and verified while preserving its existing reviews.

Why was my Google Business Profile suspended?

A Google Business Profile can be suspended for many reasons, including potential issues with the business name, address, service area, category, phone number, website, eligibility, verification, or other Google Business Profile guidelines.

Google does not always clearly explain the exact reason for a suspension.

That is why I audit the Business Profile, website, business information, and supporting evidence to identify potential compliance issues before proceeding with the reinstatement or appeal.

What do you need from me to start the GBP reinstatement process?

Getting started is simple.

I normally need Manager access to your Google Business Profile using the email address I provide, along with the supporting documents from my document checklist.

Depending on your business and situation, these may include business registration or licences, utility bills, lease documents, storefront and signage photos, invoices, branded vehicles or equipment, or other evidence showing that the business operates as represented.

You never need to share your Google account password with me.

How long does Google Business Profile reinstatement take?

I normally complete my audit, corrections, and preparation within 24 to 48 hours so there is no unnecessary delay from my side.

After that, the reinstatement timeline depends on Google.

Some suspended Google Business Profiles are reinstated within 24 hours, while others can take several days or up to 30 days. In rare and particularly complex cases, it may take 60 days or longer.

While I cannot control Google's review time, I make sure everything required from my side is handled as quickly as possible.

Can you help with Google Business Profile video verification or re verification?

Yes. I can help with Google Business Profile video verification and re verification if Google requires it during or after reinstatement.

I will guide you on what needs to be shown in the verification video and how to prepare your business before submitting it.

I may also ask you to share a video or other business evidence with me first so I can confirm that the business is legitimate and operates as represented.

Do I have to pay if my Google Business Profile is not reinstated?

No success, no payment.

For my standard Google Business Profile reinstatement service, you pay only when the agreed reinstatement work is successfully completed.

Your existing reviews are also important. I do not consider a profile fully restored if it comes back without the reviews it had before suspension.

If you have already paid and the agreed service conditions are not met, the payment is refunded according to the agreed terms.

Can you guarantee that Google will reinstate my Business Profile?

No one can guarantee a Google Business Profile reinstatement because the final decision is always made by Google.

However, if your business is legitimate, eligible for a Google Business Profile, and you can provide the necessary supporting evidence, I can review the suspension, identify potential compliance issues, make the necessary corrections, and prepare the strongest possible case for reinstatement.

Will you tell me which Google Business Profile guidelines I breached?

Yes, 100%.

My service is not just about getting your Google Business Profile reinstated.

I will explain the potential guideline or compliance issues I identify, what needed to be corrected, and what you should avoid doing in the future.

I will also guide you through the important dos and don'ts of managing your Google Business Profile after reinstatement so you can reduce the risk of another suspension.

Once my Google Business Profile is reinstated, can it get suspended again?

My approach is not limited to simply getting a suspended profile reinstated.

I work holistically by reviewing the complete Business Profile and related business information and correcting potential compliance issues. The objective is to leave you with a compliant, stable, and properly verified Google Business Profile.

However, no one can guarantee that a profile will never be suspended again.

Changes made after reinstatement to important information such as your business name, address, category, service area, website, or other profile details can trigger another review or verification. Google may also request re verification or take action because of a new issue or an account level restriction.

If your profile is suspended again because of changes made after my work is completed, an account restriction, or another new issue, it will be treated as a new case.
